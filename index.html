About the Theme

水花连众心，多元共享乐 means every splash connects us. Kuala Lumpur is home to many communities, and water is something everyone enjoys, shares and needs.

Crazy Water World: Splash of Hearts Carnival invites families with young children, youth, seniors, tourists and neighbours of every background to play, eat, watch and celebrate side by side, with one shared wish: joy for all.

Visitor Info & Safety

🎒 What to Bring

  • Swimwear and a change of clothes
  • Towel, sunscreen and a hat
  • Waterproof phone pouch (or buy one at the market)
  • Reusable water bottle

🚻 Facilities

  • Lockers and changing rooms
  • Prayer room
  • Nursing room
  • First-aid stations
  • Drinking-water stations

👶 Child Safety

  • Children wear wristbands with a parent's phone number
  • Young children must be supervised at all times
  • Lost-and-found at the main stage / exit

🔫 Water-Gun Rules

  • Never aim at the face or head
  • No homemade or modified water guns
  • No ice, hot water or hard objects
  • Use only the water guns provided

⛈️ Weather Protocol

  • If thunder or lightning is detected, water activities pause
  • Leave pools and move to shelter at once
  • Resume only when staff give the all-clear

👥 Crowds & Comfort

  • Crowd control marshals guide busy areas
  • Priority seating for seniors, pregnant visitors and families with prams
  • Food hygiene checked at all food stalls
